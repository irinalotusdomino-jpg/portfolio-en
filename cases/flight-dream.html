Flight Dream

Website for a company organizing hot-air balloon flights in Kyiv.

Challenge Convey a festive feeling and elevate the value of an unusual gift right on the hero screen, leading straight to choosing a flight.
Tools Tilda, Zero Block
Role Design and build

Solution

A dawn sky with a teapot, a little house, and birds "flying" alongside the balloon creates a fairy-tale, dreamlike mood — one that sells the emotion of flight rather than just a transport service. A single clear CTA, "Choose your flight," takes visitors straight to picking an option, with no extra steps in the way.

Result

A single-page site with a vivid, emotional hero screen, ready to receive booking requests.
