sensoryIQ

Website for the sensoryIQ children's development center in Kyiv (Varshavskyi residential complex), focused on building parents' trust and driving class bookings.

Challenge Communicate the center's values and its development programs (sensory integration, speech therapy, support for children with ASD and ADHD) and drive parents to book a class.
Tools HTML, CSS, GitHub, Netlify
Role Design and build

Solution

A soft pastel palette and a warm background create a calm, friendly mood right away, while sections on programs, class videos, and the center's space give parents a full picture before they ever pick up the phone. A booking form and contact buttons (call, Instagram) appear as standalone CTAs on every screen.

Result

A single-page landing site that presents the center, its programs, and parent testimonials, and drives visitors toward booking a class.
