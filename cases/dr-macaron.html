Dr. Macaron

Brand website for a patisserie selling handmade macarons with signature fillings.

Challenge Convey a homemade, dessert-shop feel and quickly guide visitors to order via Instagram.
Tools HTML/CSS, GitHub Pages, Netlify
Role Design and build

Solution

A warm cream-and-powder palette with a burgundy accent and a handwritten-style logo reads instantly as a patisserie rather than a generic brand. A large serif headline and a close-up macaron photo on the hero screen work like a shop window: the product and the mood are visible before visitors even scroll. A "flavor of the day" badge adds a sense of a living, constantly refreshed menu.

Result

A single-page site with a clear call to action — orders flow directly to Instagram, where the owner already has an established rapport with customers.
