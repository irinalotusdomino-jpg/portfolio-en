Pip's Orchard

A playful site for teaching English to the youngest users — letters, counting, and daily activities framed as a cozy fruit orchard.

Challenge Make learning English fun and easy for children to navigate without adult help — large buttons, a friendly mascot, simple navigation.
Tools HTML/CSS/JS, GitHub Pages
Role Design and build

Solution

The main character — Pip, a little apple wearing a crown — creates instant emotional connection and guides the child with chat-bubble lines ("Hi! I'm Pip. Let's play today!"). A warm, illustrative palette (sky, sun, an apple tree full of fruit) and a large rounded "Start exploring" CTA are sized for children's motor skills and attention span, while a "For little learners" badge tells parents right away who the site is for.

Result

A finished interactive site with playful mechanics for learning letters, counting, and a daily checklist, all woven into a single orchard-themed story.
