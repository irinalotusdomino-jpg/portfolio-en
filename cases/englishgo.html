EnglishGo

Website for a spoken-English course focused on helping learners overcome the language barrier.

Challenge Communicate the course's promise — speaking fluently and without fear — and offer two clear paths for visitors at different stages of readiness.
Tools HTML/CSS, GitHub Pages
Role Design and build

Solution

A dark purple-blue background scattered with alphabet letters symbolizes the "before learning" stage — a jumble of letters the course turns into confident speech. A bold gradient CTA, "Start speaking," contrasts with a softer outline button for a trial lesson, giving visitors a choice between committing right away and taking a low-pressure first step.

Result

A single-page site with a clear "promise → action" structure, ready to receive traffic from ads or social media.
